Almost nothing about a "wireless" call is wireless.

Your voice goes wireless for one short hop, from your phone to the nearest tower. After that it drops into cables and stays there.

The rest of the trip, sometimes across the planet, runs through fibre. A lot of it sits on the ocean floor, thick bundles of glass laid across the seabed between continents.

So when you call someone overseas, your words spend a fraction of a second in the air and the rest of the journey underwater, moving as light through a wire.

The wireless part is the exception. The wire is the whole story.
