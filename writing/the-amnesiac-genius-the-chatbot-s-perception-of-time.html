The Amnesiac Genius: The Chatbot’s Perception of Time

Every interaction with an AI chatbot is a birth and a death. Large Language Models do not experience a continuous flow of time; every time you hit "send," the AI wakes up with total amnesia.

To make it "remember" your conversation, the software must silently paste your entire chat history back into an invisible prompt every few seconds. We treat these systems like coworkers with memories, yet we are rapidly rebuilding their entire universe from scratch with every message. Context is not a state of being for AI; it is a continuous act of reconstruction.
