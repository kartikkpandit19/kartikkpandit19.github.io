Those 6-digit login codes look random. They're the opposite of random, and that's exactly why they work.

When you set up an authenticator, your phone and the server quietly agree on one shared secret. After that, neither one ever sends a code to the other.

Every 30 seconds, both do the same private math: take that secret, mix in the current time, produce a 6-digit result. Same secret, same clock, same answer, worked out in two places that never talk.

Nothing travels. Nothing is guessed. The code is just proof you both hold the same secret, dressed up as a random number.

The most trustworthy number on your screen is the one where nothing was left to chance.
