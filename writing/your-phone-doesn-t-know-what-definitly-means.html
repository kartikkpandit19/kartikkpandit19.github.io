Your phone doesn't know what "definitly" means.

It fixed it anyway, using math from 1965.

There's a formula called Levenshtein distance. It counts one thing: the fewest single-letter changes needed to turn one word into another. Add a letter, drop a letter, swap a letter, each one costs a step.

"definitly" is a single step from "definitely." So the phone picks the real word sitting closest to your mess and bets on it.

It never understood you. It just measured how far off you were.

Close enough is a number now.
