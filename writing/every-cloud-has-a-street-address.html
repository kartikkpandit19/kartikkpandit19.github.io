Every "cloud" has a street address..

They're in a warehouse, on a hard drive, in a room kept cold so the machines don't cook.

"The cloud" is one of the great rebrands in tech. It sounds soft and weightless. What's actually there is a building the size of a few football fields, running so hot that much of its power goes just to cooling itself down.

And because millions of people would lose their files if the power blinked, most of these buildings keep diesel generators outside, fueled to run for days.

Weightless technology, kept alive by the same machinery as a hospital.
