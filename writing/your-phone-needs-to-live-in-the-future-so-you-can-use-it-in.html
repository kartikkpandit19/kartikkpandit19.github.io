Your phone needs to live in the future, so you can use it in the present.

The chip inside doesn't sit and wait for your next move. It guesses. It runs ahead down the path you're most likely to take and does the work early, so the result is ready the moment you actually tap.

Guess right, and everything feels instant. Guess wrong, and it quietly throws the wasted work away before you ever see it.

So "instant" is often just a bet that paid off. The answer was worked out for a future you hadn't chosen yet.

Half of "fast" is really the phone getting there before you did.
