In Defense of the Boring: Why Finance Still Runs on 1959 Logic

In the world of high finance, "boring" is a $3 trillion virtue. While the world chases the "shiny" promise of new AI, the global banking and airline systems, the very arteries of civilization rely on COBOL, a programming language created nearly 70 years ago.

These systems remain not because of a lack of innovation, but because they work perfectly. Ripping out a proven foundation is like trying to replace the base of a skyscraper while the offices are full; the risk of a glitch is too catastrophic to entertain. Sometimes, the most beautiful software is not the one with the newest interface, but the invisible code that has quietly run the world since before we were born.
