In Defense of Elasticity: Surviving the Commodity Storm of 2026

Extreme volatility in fly-ash and slag pricing has crippled the margins of fixed-cost producers. In an era where commodity prices can swing almost 20% in a week, a static supply chain is a death sentence. The modern CEO must possess an Elastic Supply Chain.

This requires more than a list of vendors; it requires satellite-tracked logistics and real-time commodity hedging integrated into the enterprise core. You must be able to pivot raw material sourcing in hours, not months, to protect your P&L from market vagaries.

The most profitable firms treat their supply chain as a dynamic, living organism. Elasticity is the mark of a leader prepared for any outcome.
But how would this workflow look like in an AI Enabled ERP?
