The Architect vs. The Assistant: The New Technical Debt

Asking an AI to build an entire software platform by itself is a recipe for structural collapse. While AI is a brilliant assistant for writing fast snippets of code, it lacks the systemic foresight required for architecture.

It stitches together the most "mathematically probable" blocks of logic without understanding how they will interact long-term. This creates a "Technical Debt" that may work on day one but inevitably becomes an unsustainable, tangled mess as the business scales. AI can write the code, but you still require a human architect to govern the logic and prevent a structural disaster one year from now.
