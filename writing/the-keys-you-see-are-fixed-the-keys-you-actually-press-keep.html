The keys you see are fixed. The keys you actually press keep moving.

As you type, the phone predicts the next likely letter and quietly grows the tappable zone around it, while the key on screen stays exactly the same size.

Type "th" and the hidden target around "e" swells it's margins on the inside, because "the" is probably coming. Your thumb can land slightly off and still hit it.

You feel accurate. A lot of the time, the keyboard is just widening the goalposts a beat before you shoot.
