Fintech · Platform · 0→1

Quali: unified lead pipeline & smart allocator

Problem. Lead handling ran on a ₹80,000/month legacy CRM plus spreadsheets: manual qualification, weak deduplication, city-level allocation by hand. It had become the ceiling on growth.

Build. I made the call to build in-house instead of renewing the vendor, and got ops and engineering aligned on one shared definition of a good lead. The result: an API-powered engine that ingests, deduplicates, qualifies, and allocates through a business-rule engine, with operational visibility built in. AWS, PostgreSQL, MongoDB, Redis, microservices.

Result. Deduplication and qualification moved out of human judgement and into product logic; allocation became automatic.

  • 60%contribution to a 112% YoY disbursement surge
  • 75%lower cost to run than the legacy CRM
  • 1M+records processed in under 10 min
  • 9daily reports via microservice reporting
Ops tech · Geo intelligence · 0→1

Argo Sales: geo-aware appointment orchestration

Problem. Field visits were allocated by hand, ignoring pincode, distance, RM availability, and SLA. Good leads went cold in the gap between creation and visit.

Insight. The bottleneck was not lead quality. It was latency. The company was losing value because strong leads were not acted on fast enough.

Build. A geo-aware allocation engine using proximity, capacity, and SLA logic to route the right RM to the right customer automatically.

  • 17.8%increase in monthly disbursements
  • 15.9%QoQ AUM growth, with broader SLA work
  • -25%lead-to-visit time
Behavioural product · Retention

Closure Friction: turning churn intent into a signal

Problem. A loan-closure request was treated as a final event. The moment a customer asked to close, they were written off as lost.

Insight. Closure intent is not always churn. It can be a signal: a better offer wanted, a top-up, a renewal, or friction. I challenged the assumption that closure equals loss.

Build. A "Request Closure" review window that opens space for renewal, top-up, and targeted retention before anything is finalized.

  • 39.2%of closure intents recovered
  • closure → loss became closure intent → intervention → retention
CRM · Engagement · Support

Ringmaster & Titan: one operating layer for the customer

Problem. Engagement and support lived across IVR, SMS, WhatsApp, email, ticketing, and calling. RMs context-switched constantly; lifecycle outcomes were hard to measure.

Build. Ringmaster and Titan, the calling and relationship-management consoles that unified engagement across providers (Exotel, Knowlarity, Gupshup, TCN) and gave field teams a single action hub for support, collections, renewals, and top-ups. They made those teams meaningfully sharper.

  • 3.7 → 4.5Google rating from customers
  • NPS: 7.84 → 8.45 → 9.07 → 8.21 → 8.48
  • +40%RM productivity
  • -20%resolution turnaround
AI · Computer vision · Experiment

Kallu.ai: computer vision for gold valuation

Hypothesis. Use image recognition on touchstone gold streaks to estimate carat value faster and more consistently than human judgement, targeting ~90% detection accuracy under real conditions.

Approach. Define a measurable outcome, prototype fast, learn from lighting, streak, and purity variation, then decide whether the technology is production-ready. Emerging tech treated as an experiment, not a bet.
